The Arterburn 2019 Study

Two commercial weight-loss programs vs. a self-directed diet — what the data showed.

Arterburn LM, Coleman CD, Kiel J, Kelley K, Mantilla L, Frye N, Sanoshy K, Cook CM. Randomized controlled trial assessing two commercial weight loss programs in adults with overweight or obesity. Obesity Science & Practice, 2019;5(1):3–14. doi:10.1002/osp4.312 · ClinicalTrials.gov NCT02835092.

Bottom line: Over 16 weeks, structured meal-replacement programs produced ~25× more weight loss than a self-directed reduced-calorie diet — and people who got more coaching lost more than 2× as much weight as those who got less.

Study at a Glance

  • Design: 16-week, 3-arm randomized controlled trial. Single site (Chicago), 2016–2017.
  • Participants: 198 adults (81% women, mean age 46, mean BMI 34.2). 92% completed.
  • The 3 arms:
    • OPTAVIA 5&1 (with coaching) — 5 fuelings + 1 lean & green meal/day, 23 prescribed coaching calls.
    • Medifast 4&2&1 (self-guided) — 4 meal replacements + 2 lean & green + 1 snack, no structured coaching.
    • Control — self-directed reduced-calorie diet (USDA MyPlate), one 10–15 minute intro session.

Both programs vs. control: p < 0.0001.

It Wasn't Just Weight — It Was the Right Weight

  • Fat mass (DXA): OPTAVIA lost 4.5 kg of fat (−10.8% of fat mass). Medifast lost 3.75 kg (−8.2%). Control: −0.22 kg.
  • Visceral (belly) fat: OPTAVIA −239 g, Medifast −236 g, Control −8 g.
  • Waist circumference: OPTAVIA −6.2 cm, Medifast −5.2 cm, Control −2.3 cm.
  • Hip circumference: OPTAVIA −4.1 cm, Medifast −3.6 cm, Control −0.3 cm.
  • Inflammation (hsCRP): Significantly lower in OPTAVIA completers vs. control (−1.33 vs. 0.00 mg/L).

The Coaching Effect

  • OPTAVIA participants attended a mean of 16.1 of 23 prescribed coaching calls.
  • Those who completed ≥17 calls lost 6.9 kg; those completing fewer lost only 3.1 kg — 2.3× more weight with more coaching.
  • 83.3% of those who used the discretionary coaching network achieved ≥5% loss vs. 34.6% who didn't (p = 0.002).
Support isn't a "nice to have" — in this trial, it was the single biggest predictor of who lost weight and who didn't.

Safety

  • No serious adverse events were related to the interventions. (4 SAEs across all groups: knee surgery, tonsillectomy, renal failure in MED, transient ischemic attack in control — none judged related.)
  • Mild GI events (looser bowels, bloating, constipation) more common on the meal-replacement plans, all mild–moderate.

Honest Limitations (so you don't get cornered on Q&A)

  • Open-label — participants knew which group they were in.
  • Single Chicago site; generalizability beyond that population is limited.
  • Study coaches lacked the experience of real-world OPTAVIA coaches; commercial users also have a community network this study deliberately stripped out — so real-world results could plausibly run higher or lower.
  • Funded by Medifast, Inc. (disclosed). Independent peer-reviewed publication.

What to Say On Stage

"This isn't a story about willpower. It's a story about structure and support. Without those, in 16 weeks, the average person lost less than half a pound."
"With structured meals plus coaching, more than 1 in 4 lost 10% or more of their body weight. That's not a tweak — that's a different person walking out of the room."
"The people who showed up to coaching lost more than twice as much as the people who didn't. That's the part nobody wants to hear and everybody needs to hear."
