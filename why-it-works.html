Three Faces, One Problem

Diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol — these aren't three different diseases. They're three windows looking into the same room.

The room is insulin resistance — cells that have stopped responding to insulin properly. It's the engine driving most chronic disease in adults today.

🩸Diabetes / pre-diabetesInsulin resistance → blood sugar rises → A1c climbs
💢High blood pressureInsulin resistance → sodium retention + vessel stiffening → BP rises
🩺High cholesterolInsulin resistance → triglycerides up, HDL down, LDL shifts to small dense particles (the kind that actually predicts heart disease)

When someone tells me they have diabetes and high blood pressure and high cholesterol, I don't think "three problems." I think "one problem with three faces."

What "Metabolically Healthy" Actually Means

You need all five of these in the optimal range — and not be on medication for any of them:

  • Waist < 40 in (men) · < 35 in (women)
  • Fasting glucose < 100 · A1c < 5.7%
  • Blood pressure < 120 / 80
  • Triglycerides < 150
  • HDL ≥ 40 (men) · ≥ 50 (women)

Miss any one, and you're not metabolically healthy by this definition. Only 1 in 200 adults with obesity meets all five.

Araújo J, Cai J, Stevens J. Prevalence of Optimal Metabolic Health in American Adults: NHANES 2009–2016. Metabolic Syndrome and Related Disorders, 2019;17(1):46–52.

The Coaching Effect

Inside the OPTAVIA arm, people who completed 17 or more coaching calls lost 2.3× more weight (6.9 kg) than those who completed fewer (3.1 kg).

Support isn't a "nice to have." In this trial, it was the single biggest predictor of who lost weight and who didn't.

Arterburn LM et al. Randomized controlled trial assessing two commercial weight loss programs in adults with overweight or obesity. Obesity Science & Practice, 2019;5(1):3–14.
